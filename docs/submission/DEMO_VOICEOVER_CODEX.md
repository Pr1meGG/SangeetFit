One photo. One lehenga. Here is the original—and the look generated by YouCam.

For an Indian wedding, choosing what to wear can mean exploring very different
styles for Sangeet, Mehendi, Haldi, the wedding and the reception.

SangeetFit brings those outfit ideas into one place, with a fitting room to help
you explore a look on your photo.

Choose an occasion, browse the labelled AI outfit ideas, and shortlist the ones
you like. You can also add a garment you already own. Adding clothes never
starts an AI task.

When you want a preview, open the fitting room, review the photo notice, add a
full-body photo, and press Generate.

This recorded live run uses an AI-generated demo person. The server sends the
photo and garment to YouCam Cloth V4, then checks for the finished result. This
generation took twenty-six seconds; the wait shown here is shortened.

Move the slider to compare the original with the generated look. You can inspect
the result, compare recent previews and save or open an image you want to keep.

It is visual guidance, not a guarantee of physical fit. Check your face and the
garment details before making a decision.

SangeetFit. Explore a look, preview it, and choose what feels like you.
