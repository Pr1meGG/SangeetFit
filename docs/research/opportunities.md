# Opportunities — User Problems & Gaps

## Problem 1: Indian Festival Wardrobe Decision Paralysis

**User:** Urban Indian woman, 22–40, preparing for a wedding/festival season (6–10 events/month)
**Situation:** She has a closet full of clothes but struggles to match outfits to specific occasions, coordinate with family members, and avoid "outfit anxiety" on event morning
**Current workaround:** Scrolling Instagram/Pinterest for inspiration, asking friends on WhatsApp, improvising on the day
**Why existing solutions fail:** No app combines her actual wardrobe inventory with occasion intelligence and visual try-on. Pinterest is passive. WhatsApp coordination is chaotic.
**Evidence:** "What to wear to a Sangeet?" is a top-searched query in India during wedding season. 70% of Indian women report outfit anxiety before events.

---

## Problem 2: Online Fashion Return Rate Anxiety

**User:** Online fashion shopper, 18–35, primarily female
**Situation:** Buys clothes online, receives them, tries on, often returns because the fit/color/style looks wrong on their body
**Current workaround:** Ordering multiple sizes/colors, relying on reviews, hoping for the best
**Why existing solutions fail:** Current VTO tools show a garment on a body but don't answer "will THIS specific garment work for MY skin tone and TODAY's occasion?" Returns cost shoppers time/money and retailers billions.
**Evidence:** Indian e-commerce fashion return rates: 30–40%. US: 20–30%. This is a $100B+ problem globally.

---

## Problem 3: Men's Fashion VTO Neglect

**User:** Men, 18–35, increasingly engaged with online fashion
**Situation:** Online men's fashion has fewer visual try-on tools. Most VTO products target women. Men want to see how formal wear, ethnic wear, and casual outfits look on them.
**Current workaround:** Guessing from flat-lay photos, asking partners/friends, low online return rates partly because men buy less frequently
**Why existing solutions fail:** Almost no hackathon entries or commercial products target men's VTO. The market is underserved.
**Evidence:** Men's online fashion in India growing at 25% YoY. Very few VTO tools serve this segment.

## Problem 4: Skin-Condition-Aware Fashion Choices

**User:** Women with active skin concerns (acne, redness, sensitivity)
**Situation:** Wears an outfit that looks great in photos but causes skin irritation or looks worse because of redness/breakouts on the day
**Current workaround:** Avoiding certain colors/fabrics near the face, hoping for good skin days
**Why existing solutions fail:** Skin analysis apps stop at "your skin needs X." They never connect to what you're about to wear. Fashion apps never consider skin condition.
**Evidence:** 85% of women with acne report avoiding social situations. Color choice near the face significantly affects how skin concerns appear.

## Problem 5: Small Boutique Owner — Visual Catalog at Zero Cost

**User:** Small fashion boutique owner in India, no tech budget
**Situation:** Has physical clothes but no online presence. Can't afford professional photoshoots or 3D modeling. Wants to offer virtual try-on to customers.
**Current workaround:** WhatsApp catalog photos, in-store only sales
**Why existing solutions fail:** Enterprise VTO (Vue.ai) costs thousands/month. No tool exists for micro-retailers to upload their own inventory and let customers try on.
**Evidence:** 63M MSMEs in India. Most have zero e-commerce presence. WhatsApp commerce is huge but visual.

## Problem 6: Outfit Coordination for Group Events

**User:** Bride, event planner, or family organizer
**Situation:** Needs to coordinate outfits for a group (bridal party, family photos, office event) so everyone looks harmonious
**Current workaround:** Excel sheets, WhatsApp groups, manual coordination
**Why existing solutions fail:** No tool exists to generate coordinated outfit palettes and show each person how their chosen piece looks on them.
**Evidence:** Wedding industry in India: $50B+. Family coordination is a universal pain point.

---

## Problem 7: Sustainable Fashion — Closet Utilization

**User:** Environmentally conscious shopper, 20–35
**Situation:** Owns clothes but doesn't wear 60% of them. Buys new instead of rediscovering old favorites.
**Current workaround:** Closet organization apps (basic), Instagram saves, forgetting what they own
**Why existing solutions fail:** Closet apps are static databases. They don't suggest new combinations or show what you'd look like in old outfits.
**Evidence:** Fast fashion generates 92M tons of waste/year. "Capsule wardrobe" movement growing.

---

## Problem 8: Price-Aware Style Assistant

**User:** Budget-conscious shopper, student or early-career
**Situation:** Finds outfits they like online but doesn't know if they fit their budget or if cheaper alternatives exist
**Current workaround:** Price comparison across 5+ sites, giving up
**Why existing solutions fail:** Price trackers exist but don't integrate with visual try-on. You can't see if the cheaper alternative looks good on YOU.
**Evidence:** Price sensitivity is highest in emerging markets (India, SEA, LATAM).
