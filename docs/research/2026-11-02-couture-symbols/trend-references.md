# 4. Current references (2024–2026): curated evidence, not a measured trend survey

Checked 2 November 2026. These are 15 specific designer-authored references. Fourteen have verified publication dates in the requested window; the last has an unverified date. All 15 URLs resolve to matching indexed/direct project content, but access varies by tool. Behance pages that returned 403 to the text fetcher were opened successfully in the public browser. The table distinguishes actual visual inspection from author-description evidence. No small-size logo tests were performed.

**Coverage limitation:** Dribbble and Behance yielded attributable projects. Public Cosmos search required sign-up, X search redirected to login, and Pinterest showed a signed-out login modal without attributable results. These three platforms were searched but are **not represented by invented posts or generic collection links**. Consequently this sample cannot establish cross-platform prevalence or a 2024→2026 change in popularity.

## What this sample supports

1. **Craft-led ownership is more useful than generic luxury decoration.** The Svelte, Dharaa and Raw India author accounts ground identity in a particular craft or place. They are three different projects, but all are self-published portfolio sources; this is a supported sample observation, not a universal consumer-response finding.
2. **A calm core can coexist with cultural richness elsewhere.** The Raw India, Bandhan and Dharaa show/described broader illustration, imagery or narrative systems alongside logos. SangeetFit's symbol does not need to carry the entire wedding.
3. **Flat one-color artwork and material presentation must be evaluated separately.** Svelte, Reflections and TRAUM demonstrate why embossing/foil-like scenes are persuasive at presentation size; none establishes the proposed SangeetFit symbol's small-size performance.
4. **'Luxury' is an unreliable search qualifier.** The corpus mixes client projects, named brand exercises and explicitly saleable personal concepts. Publication dates, likes, featured status and author adjectives do not establish commercial success or originality.

## Adversarial checks

- A coherent one-color mark may outperform a more elaborate cultural emblem; the Indrakosh/Divine comparison illustrates the tradeoff, not a measured winner.
- A Didone, gold and dark background can make almost any presentation feel fashion-adjacent. Remove photography, texture and embossing before judging distinctiveness.
- This is a convenience sample skewed toward accessible English-language portfolio pages. No claim about the most popular motif, trend growth, cultural universality or sales impact is justified.
- Revisit Pinterest, Cosmos and X only if access allows creator/date/source attribution; a repost's save date must not be mistaken for creation date.
