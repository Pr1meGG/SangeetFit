# SangeetFit couture-symbol research plan

Date: 2 November 2026. Deliverable: a sourced, text-only designer handoff with the seven requested research sections, five concept briefs, and ranked top two recommendations.

## Locked brief

Sangeet celebration; symbol plus name; luxury couture personality; `SANGEET` in wide-tracked Didone capitals plus flowing script `Fit`. Symbol must support a flat single-colour rendition and a recognisable 16 px version. The newest brief supersedes the October logo exploration for this research; existing app assets are outside scope.

The supplied board describes A Ghera (12 alternating skirt panels), B Ghungroo (five graduated bells), C Toran (scallops and mango leaves). Treat these descriptions as supplied facts. Do not claim visual inspection unless the actual artifact can be opened.

## Decision and falsifiable hypotheses

1. A fabric-spin concept can express sangeet and clothing more specifically than a generic celebration flower. Counterevidence: it reads as a mandala, fan, or aperture at small sizes.
2. A ghungroo concept can carry music/dance while retaining a hallmark silhouette. Counterevidence: bells and fine cord merge or read as generic jewellery at 16 px.
3. A toran can bridge celebration and adornment. Counterevidence: it communicates doorway decoration, leaves, or a crown without music/dance.
4. Didone plus script can retain a couture hierarchy if scripts are restrained and optically balanced. Counterevidence: hairlines, excessive flourishes, and tiny Fit lose recognition.

## Research blocks and ownership

- Indian brand identities: independent stream; primary brand/designer sources; 12–15 entries.
- Global identities and fonts: independent stream; 8–10 brands plus six verified Google Fonts.
- 2024–2026 references: independent stream; 15 specific works, searched across all five requested platforms; explicit date/access limits.
- Primary session: cultural vocabulary (20+), pitfalls, three palettes, five briefs, source index, evidence review, final integration.

All files are confined to this new research folder. No edits to existing assets, product code, project-state documents, or other current work. No live provider tasks, deployments, commits, pushes, generated images, or publishing.

## Sourcing and evidence standard

Official brand histories establish documented meanings; creator case studies establish design intent; museum, cultural, and academic sources establish cultural context. Construction and luxury fit are analyst judgments, explicitly labelled. Scores are comparative design judgments, not measured uniqueness or trademark clearance. Do not require three sources for simple official self-description; strategic theses require multiple independent sources or an explicit uncertainty label.

Save each source in `sources/` with URL, date, a short evidence excerpt, relevance, credibility/recency/bias scores, and limits. Keep source excerpts short. Distinguish link resolution, readable content, and actual visual inspection. No invented dates or origin stories.

## Opposition searches and risks

Search rosette/mandala/aperture overlap, ghungroo versus generic jingle bells, toran versus necklace/crown, couture sans-serif counterexamples, Didone hairline failure, and stock-logo repetition. A visual metaphor is not exclusive ownership. Avoid treating regional dance and wedding symbols as universal Indian tradition. Platform portfolios are curated examples, not market-frequency evidence.

## Acceptance and stop criteria

All seven sections present; requested counts met or a specific evidence shortfall disclosed; direct URLs for each reference; tables for 1, 2, 3, 7; all five briefs include construction, small-size simplification, lockup, references, risks; ranked top two; no generated images. Save report, source index, findings, and refresh targets. Audit URLs/attributions and disclose inaccessible artifacts. Text-only work needs document/claim checks, not an app build.
