## Synthesis for the locked SangeetFit brief

1. **A hallmark is a better benchmark than wedding stationery.** Tanishq's original designer account, Sabyasachi's insignia products and Valaya's Phoenix hardware independently show that a symbol can be an object worth wearing. For Ghungroo, ask whether the silhouette could become a metal charm or embroidered badge before adding fine cord decoration.
2. **Simple geometry can carry layered cultural meaning.** Nicobar's designer account is the clearest example. This supports a Toran construction in which the curve/pendant rhythm reads first and mango leaves are the second reading. It does not prove the proposed construction will be original or legible.
3. **Do not turn symbol choice into a claimed luxury formula.** Raw Mango and Gaurav Gupta show that cultural and couture authority need not come from animal crests or Didone capitals. Keep the locked wordmark as a brief constraint; do not claim the surveyed market proves it is the only luxury choice.
4. **Ghera's strongest risk is rosette convergence.** Amrapali's lotus and Good Earth's sun-like flourish already occupy adjacent visual territory. Twelve equal radial divisions plus alternating colour can easily become a generic floral wheel when made monochrome. Preserve a recognisable fabric overlap or displaced rotational gap, then prove it at 16px.

## Adversarial check

The visible identities are not controlled evidence that a logo creates luxury perception. Recognition, product quality, craft, price positioning, environments and repetition contribute. None of these sources proves a new SangeetFit symbol is legally original, culturally universal, or readable at 16px. Those remain designer prototype/similarity checks. In particular, do not describe the existing artifact as visually verified on the strength of this research.

Skipped: exact proprietary font identification and speculative emblem-origin stories. Risk: several brands have multiple logo/product versions; compare the final SangeetFit geometry with the relevant current marks, not just their names.
