### Reusable principles and counterevidence

1. **Material logic is fertile:** LOEWE's branding irons, CELINE's chain and Valentino's buckle application support studying a bell's opening, a skirt's seam or a toran's suspension. These three primary cases support a useful design hypothesis, not a measured law of luxury perception.
2. **One unit, exact relationships:** Fendi, LOEWE and CHANEL illustrate concentration on a small number of recognizable relationships. Repetition and restraint are transferable; their actual silhouettes are not.
3. **Do not equate luxury with tiny detail:** Versace is the counterexample to compulsory minimalism. Its fame and repeated exposure help people recognize a complex emblem. A new 16 px web mark has less recognition equity and must be tested on its own.
4. **Historical precision matters:** Valentino accounts differ on 1967 versus 1968 and may refer to different uses; avoid asserting a single launch date for the present master drawing. CHANEL origin legends are unnecessary to the useful principle. Current brand forms can change; these links establish specific examples rather than an eternal master identity.

## 6. Typography check

**Retain the locked Didone plus script direction, with conditions.** The contrast can express the intended structured couture name and celebratory movement. This is an interpretive fit, not proof that the pairing is unique or perceived as luxury by users. Both high-contrast styles can become fragile together, and a small subordinate “Fit” can disappear or read as an unrelated signature. The joining and optical spacing need designer work.

### Wordmark conditions for the designer

- Keep the brand read as **SangeetFit**, one name, even when the typography distinguishes the two parts. The visual spacing should not imply a second unrelated brand called Fit.
- Treat wide tracking as a starting direction, then optically adjust uneven pairs in SANGEET. Do not letter-space a connecting script by default.
- Give Fit enough visible body height to read at the intended header size. Keep its capital F clear of the final T and the symbol's edges; the dot on i and crossbar on t must survive.
- Match the script's darkest stroke to the Didone's visual weight. Two incompatible hairline systems can look accidental even when both fonts are attractive individually.
- Use the standalone symbol at 16 px. The requirement that a symbol works at 16 px does not imply that the full SANGEET Fit lockup must be legible at 16 px tall.
- Ask for one-color and reversed proofs, actual 16/24/32/40 px symbol rasters, and wordmark proofs at real header dimensions. Embroidery and foil need vendor-specific minimum stroke/spacing checks; a pixel threshold is not a manufacturing specification.

No letterforms have been drawn or raster-tested in this research stream. Font availability and documented characteristics are verified; final pairing quality, visual similarity and production fitness remain design/proof decisions.
