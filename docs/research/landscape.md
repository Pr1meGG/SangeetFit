### Gap Analysis
- **Temporal progress tracking + VTO combined**: No entry connects skin progress over time WITH outfit changes
- **Occasion-based styling with skin-awareness**: Festival/ceremony wear is culturally specific but underexplored for Indian market
- **Decision support beyond "here's your outfit"**: Most entries show results but don't help users *decide*
- **Multi-API orchestration with reasoning**: Only 2-3 entries use 3+ APIs; none combine skin analysis → temporal tracking → VTO → decision engine
- **Accessibility beyond audio**: Aloud won on accessibility but only for skin; no VTO accessibility work exists
- **Indian market specificity**: Festive wear, skin tone inclusivity for South Asian tones, family occasion coordination

## Commercial Products Solving Similar Problems
- **YouCam Makeup** (Perfect Corp): Individual API features, no orchestrated product
- **TrueFit, Zeekit (Walmart)**: VTO only, no skin AI integration
- **Perfect Corp's beauty brands**: Point solutions, not end-to-end experiences
- **StyleSnap (Stitch Fix)**: Style recommendation without skin analysis
- **Hairstyle apps**: Hair-focused only, no full-body VTO + skin pipeline

## User Problem Evidence
- 67% of online fashion returns due to fit/color mismatch (Clothing Online Return Rate Study 2024)
- Indian festive wear market: $8B+ annually, mostly offline purchase due to color/tradition uncertainty
- 73% of women with darker skin tones report foundations/makeup not matching their skin (Journal of Clinical and Aesthetic Dermatology)
- Blind/low-vision population in India: 4.5 million (WHO 2024)
