# SangeetFit — Premium Logo Iterations

Two refined directions built from the **25 October 2026 premium exploration**.

## Direction A · Drape S *(recommended)*

Iteration on **Direction II (Dupatta)** — the fabric-drape S.

**Original insight:** One hairline falling like fabric from a shoulder, resolving into an S. Closest to the product story (Indian wedding wardrobe) but became a squiggle at small sizes.

**What changed:**
- Bolder primary stroke (5 px vs 2.6) — now legible at 16 px
- Added inner fine stroke — brings the Didone high-contrast couture feel
- Clearer S silhouette — reads as an S at any size, not a squiggle
- Retains the **gold dot** as the rhythm signature

**Symbol** → reads as: fabric fold, letter S, dancer's movement, treble-clef curve

## Direction B · Kundan S

Iteration on **Direction III (Kundan)** — the marquise gem.

**Original insight:** A kundan stone silhouette with concentric bezel and gold seed. Held shape at small sizes but said "gem" not "wardrobe."

**What changed:**
- Split the marquise into two lobes that form the upper/lower curves of an S
- Added a subtle S-bridge connecting the lobes
- Central gold seed becomes the note/beat
- Now reads as both a jewel and an S

## Shared Elements

Both directions share the **Oct 2026 premium vocabulary:**
- **One gold dot** — the brand's rhythm signature
- **High-contrast Didone strokes** — thin/thick for couture feel
- **Palette:** Burgundy `#5B1730` · Gold `#B08D4F` · Ivory `#F7F1E5` · Paper `#FBF7F1`
- **No enclosing disc, no paisley cliché, no gradient, no body silhouette**
- **Wordmark:** "Sangeet" in serif (Bodoni style) + "Fit" in light sans-serif
